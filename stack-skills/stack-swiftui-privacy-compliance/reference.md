# SwiftUI (iOS): Privacy and compliance

Stack-specific implementation for **SwiftUI (iOS)** (Mobile). Normative rules: domain skill `security-privacy-compliance` / `reference.md`.

## Data subject rights

**Sub-topics:** Erasure, Export, Rectification, Threat modeling and trust boundaries (Data subject rights), Abuse cases and adversarial testing (Data subject rights)

### Do (use this in SwiftUI (iOS))

- Store tokens in Keychain with `kSecAttrAccessibleAfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly`.
- Use ATS (HTTPS).

### Don't

- Do not store secrets in UserDefaults.
- Do not disable ATS globally.

### Verify

- MobSF scan on IPA.
